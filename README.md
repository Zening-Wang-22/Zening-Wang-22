## 🔬 Research

**Rice University**  
I am co-advised by **[Prof. Marina Vannucci](https://marina.blogs.rice.edu/)** and **[Prof. Cindy Zhang](https://profiles.rice.edu/faculty/cindy-zhang)**. My research focuses on Bayesian transfer learning and causal inference, particularly Mendelian randomization and borrowing information across populations.

**Duke University**  
Previously, I worked with **[Prof. Anru Zhang](https://anruzhang.github.io/index.html)** on matrix and tensor factorization methods to integrate longitudinal clinical data with static patient characteristics for sepsis phenotyping.
